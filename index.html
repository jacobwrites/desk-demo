          What it is, and what it isn't

          It does
          • Matches new CVs to open roles, and new roles to people on file
          • Reads and files CVs and call notes
          • Keeps each role and the pipeline current
          • Flags who needs a reply, who has gone quiet, and missing consent
          • Drafts documents and emails for you to edit
          • Keeps a note of your corrections and applies them
          • Researches companies and pay from public sources
          It never
          • Sends an email or message
          • Contacts a candidate or a client
          • Searches or scrapes LinkedIn
          • Makes a decision about a person. It suggests; you decide
          • Replaces your CRM. It sits beside whatever you use

          What it can't see: WhatsApp, LinkedIn messages, and anything that isn't written down.

          Candidate files stay on your computer. Text is processed by Claude under your own account to do the work. So far it has only been run on Gmail on a Mac; anything else gets tested first.
